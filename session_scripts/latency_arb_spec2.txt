Anti-Fraud System - Latency Arbitrage 
Module​
Executive Objective​
Transform the existing CRM Anti-Fraud module from a fragmented, predominantly manual 
analytical system into a holistic, deterministic and event-driven detection, decision-making and 
control framework, using Latency Arbitrage as the first priority use case. The existing AI/ML 
behavioural profiling capability will be calibrated in parallel and integrated into the Risk 
decision workflow.​

Expected Outcome​
At the end of three weeks, the organisation should have a validated first version of a modern 
Anti-Fraud workflow, with Latency Arbitrage as the priority capability. The deliverable is 
therefore broader than a detection model: it is the foundation for moving the CRM Anti-Fraud 
module from fragmented manual analysis toward integrated detection, decision-making and 
controlled automation for risk management actions.​
