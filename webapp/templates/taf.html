Walk-forward — we predict the week before the money moves

Each week is scored by a model trained only on prior weeks; the operating threshold is calibrated on a held-out validation window, never on the test weeks. Bars = that week's perfect-oracle abuse (the upper bound); the gold line = what the model flagged a week ahead and we would have captured.
