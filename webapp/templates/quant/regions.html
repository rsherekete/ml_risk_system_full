Firm P&L and accounts by client geography, not by server.

Why region rather than server

A server says which machine an account lives on — that is plumbing. Region is the dimension a business question is actually asked in, and it is what a concentration or compliance question means. The raw country field needed normalising first: CN and China were separate values, as were MY/Malaysia, so aggregating it as stored split single markets in two.
