Cash Flow

Deposits, withdrawals and transfers — overall, by region, and by account.

Why this matters beyond accounting

Cash movement is the strongest fraud signal in the dataset and is invisible to any model reading only trades. Money in, a token trade, money out leaves no trading footprint at all. Internal transfers are separated from external funding: counting them as deposits double-counts firm-wide inflow, because the matching withdrawal from the other account is also recorded.
