How to read this

Separate from routing on purpose. Routing asks whether an account is worth hedging; this asks whether it warrants a phone call — an arbitrageur and a skilled client may both deserve an A-book, but only one deserves investigation. Every flag states its measurement, the threshold crossed, and the number of trades behind it, because a surveillance alert is an accusation and should be judged rather than trusted. Nothing is flagged on fewer than 50 closed trades.
