How this list was formed

A routing decision is made before the session, when nobody knows who will trade. So the candidate set is every account with data on or before {{ selected_day }}, reduced to its most recent observation and carried forward — {{ '{:,}'.format(population) }} accounts, excluding those dormant more than 30 days. {% if outcome %} Of them, {{ '{:,}'.format(outcome.active_accounts) }} actually traded that day and {{ outcome.hedged_active }} of the hedged names were among them — a hedge on an account that stays flat neither costs nor earns anything.{% endif %}

Hedge list — {{ selected_day }}

Ranked by expected dollar impact, not raw probability: a confident call on a tiny account is worth nothing, so the consequential names come first.
