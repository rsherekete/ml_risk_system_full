Risk measurement note

VaR here is historical, not parametric. Client P&L is violently heavy-tailed — the top 1% of winning account-days carry roughly 40% of all winner dollars — so a normal approximation would report a comfortable number precisely on the days that matter. These are empirical quantiles of the actual daily series over {{ risk.var.days if risk.var else 0 }} days, with expected shortfall (the average loss given the threshold is breached) beside each one.

Rolling risk

Cumulative P&L against rolling volatility and 30-day VaR. A profitable stretch that was violent is a different book from a calm one at the same total.
