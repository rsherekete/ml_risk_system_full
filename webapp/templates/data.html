Why refresh is incremental

Two years across six servers is far too much to re-query whenever the dashboard wants fresh numbers. Each server is read from its own watermark minus a {{ overlap_days }}-day overlap, and rows are de-duplicated on trade identity keeping the newest — so an amended or late-closing trade replaces the stored copy instead of being missed or duplicated. Only the months a fetch touches are rewritten.

Source precedence

MySQL is primary — it is the system of record and costs nothing per query. BigQuery is the fallback, used only where MySQL has no copy: mt5_dubai_live01 does not exist in this MySQL instance and remains a BigQuery source. Kafka supplies live flow for today, and is never mixed with settled warehouse figures — the two are shown separately because a stale live number is more dangerous than an absent one.
