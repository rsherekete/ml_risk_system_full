Accuracy & edge

  • Model accuracy is the share of closed trades whose realized sign matched the model's expectation — the strategy doing what it predicted.
  • Expected (honest) is the model's pre-trade dollar forecast with the lot-calibration multiplier divided back out — a number realized P&L can fairly be held against.
  • Client @ our size is the client's own realized P&L on each mirrored trade, scaled to our lots (copy = same sign, invert/fade = opposite) — the zero-cost, zero-slippage perfect-mirror ceiling. Uses the client's real close event where recorded.
  • Our edge vs client = our realized − client-at-our-size, computed ONLY on trades joined to the client's real close (count shown). Negative = execution drag (late exits, slippage, early capacity closes); positive = our entries/exits beat pure mirroring.
